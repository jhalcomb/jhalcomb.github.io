App privacy policy

WordSearch Rally
Privacy Policy.

How WordSearch Rally handles information during gameplay and support interactions.

Effective date: November 11, 2026 · Last updated: November 11, 2026

jHal Labs respects your privacy. This policy describes the information handled by WordSearch Rally (the “App”), how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information and accounts

We do not require you to create an account to use the App. The App is designed to work without a jHal Labs account or a jHal Labs-hosted gameplay server.

If you choose a display name or nickname in the App, that name may be shown to other participants in a multiplayer session. Please avoid using sensitive personal information as a display name.

2. Local multiplayer

To enable nearby multiplayer gameplay, the App may exchange gameplay information directly between participating devices. This can include word-search boards, found words, completion status, and session standings. Other players in the same session may see shared gameplay or player-status information.

We do not operate a remote gameplay server that receives or stores this session information. Devices and their operating systems may process network information as needed to establish local connections.

3. Data stored on your device

The App may store word-search puzzles, words found, and match progress, along with preferences or settings, on your device. This information is used to provide gameplay features and is not uploaded to a jHal Labs account.

4. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not sell personal information.

5. Apple services

Apple may separately process information related to App Store downloads, purchases, crash reports, or device services under Apple's Privacy Policy. Those practices are controlled by Apple, not by jHal Labs.

6. Support messages

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to include. We use that information to respond to your request and keep support records for as long as reasonably needed to provide support or comply with applicable obligations. We do not use support messages for advertising.

7. Children's privacy

The App is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Parents and guardians should help children choose non-identifying display names when playing with others.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not maintain online gameplay accounts or store multiplayer session data on our servers. You can generally remove the App's locally stored data by deleting the App from your device; any data retained in device backups is managed through your Apple settings. To request deletion of correspondence you sent to our support address, please contact us using the details below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The updated version will be posted here with a revised effective or last-updated date.
