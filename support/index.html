Frequently asked questions

How do I play with friends?

Our Rally games are designed around nearby multiplayer sessions. Open the same game on participating devices and follow its host/join instructions. If prompted, allow the local-network access needed to connect.

Which devices are supported?

NineRally Sudoku and WordSearch Rally are designed for iPhone and iPad. Word Scramble Rally is currently designed for iPhone. Availability and compatibility details will appear on each game's App Store listing.

Where can I read the privacy policies?

All our game-specific privacy policies are listed on the privacy page.

Can I suggest a feature or report a bug?

Absolutely. Send an email with the game name and a short description of your idea or the issue you encountered.
